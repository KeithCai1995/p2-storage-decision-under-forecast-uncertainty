# Responsible use and scope note

The included forecast and market data are simulated. Results must not be attributed to
PetroChina, Tarim Oilfield, a named market, or an operational trading desk. The schedules
are not financial advice and must not be deployed without market-rule validation,
engineering review, safety constraints, degradation modelling and live data controls.

The repository includes the configurations, output snapshots and tests needed to inspect
the SOC equations, reproduce the metrics and verify that the oracle is used only as a
diagnostic upper bound.

Experiment dates and version-control history must remain factual. If a university,
supervisor or application form asks about generative AI, coding assistance or external
editing, that assistance should be disclosed accurately under the applicable rule.
